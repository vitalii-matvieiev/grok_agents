# AgentOS Violet-Only Palette

## Goal

Remove the remaining turquoise and green styling from Matvieiev AgentOS so that violet is the only color that communicates system activity, interaction, and live routing.

## Palette rules

- Violet and light violet replace turquoise in buttons, active states, focus indicators, map routes, pulse animations, hover treatments, and the ready indicator.
- Gold remains exclusively for human governance, approval, and control: Vitalii, approval language, and related status accents.
- Graphite surfaces, warm paper text, and neutral muted text remain unchanged.
- No copy, layout, routes, CTA destinations, or application behaviour changes.

## Verification

- Search the stylesheet for the previous turquoise and green color tokens and replace all active visual usages.
- Confirm the map's ready dot, route animation, active nodes, buttons, and keyboard focus are violet.
- Confirm governance remains gold and contrast remains readable on graphite.
- Check the public page after deployment.
